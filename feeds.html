CVE-2026-133001

Published: 2026-11-19 05:17:08 CDT

Severity: N/A

CVSS Score: N/A

FlowIntel contains an authorization flaw in its duplicate alert ingestion logic. When a duplicate alert is received and its content is updated, the ownership and visibility scope fields (owner user and owner organization) were not refreshed to reflect the connector's current scope. As a result, an alert that was originally created under a broader visibility scope retained that broader scope even after the connector's scope was narrowed or changed. This means that users or organizations that should no longer have visibility into a given alert could continue to access it, because the stale ownership fields were never updated during the duplicate-update path. The issue affected all duplicate updates, including those involving alerts in terminal review states and those recovered from uniqueness conflicts. Security impact: - Unauthorized disclosure of security alert content to users or organizations outside the intended visibility scope. - Stale access persists indefinitely until the alert is independently re-scoped or deleted. Affected version: <4.1.0.
